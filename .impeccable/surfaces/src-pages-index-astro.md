# Surface: marketing site (Home first, then the rest of the pages in the same world)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: real estate agents and firms in Delhi NCR and Uttarakhand, mostly on phones. Action: send an enquiry (phone-first form) or open WhatsApp. Proof: sample renders, clearly labelled, until the owner supplies real footage. No invented stats, clients or testimonials.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is a blue-hour property shoot. Every listing looks best in the twenty minutes after sunset, and Veylo works in that hour. It refuses the category default, a black page with neon glow and a row of icon cards.
OWN-WORLD: The sky runs from deep navy (#0A1630) at the top to black (#05060A) at the ground. A glass tower has its windows lit warm-white and cool. The electric-blue swoosh (#1F7BFF to #59A8FF) is a single sweeping arc taken from the logo, and it carries the eye between sections. Silver wide-tracked caps (Michroma) form the wall lettering, with Manrope for body text. The rounded "band" bar comes from the brand graphic, and its blue corner block is the primary action. Icons are thin 1.5px blue line icons.
STORY: Visitors see their listings could look like this, understand that Veylo does shoots, drone, social, content and influencer work for agents and firms in their area, and then enquire or tap WhatsApp.
FIRST VIEWPORT: A full-bleed tower render fills the right 60% and bleeds off the top. The headline sits lower-left in silver caps across 3 lines. The blue arc sweeps from bottom-left behind the headline into the tower. Along the bottom, a band-form holds Name | Phone | Agent/Firm, with a blue corner block "Get my free content plan" as the submit button. WhatsApp is a ghost button above the band.
FORM: Blue hour, the top-ranked grounded candidate, chosen by the owner ("choose based on my theme"). seed: 87af23cc4766e2fe. The roll ran degraded; the owner pinned the direction over assigned candidate 4 (sales gallery).
SIGNATURE: Lights on. As you scroll, the hero tower crossfades from dusk to lights on, using CSS scroll-driven animation with no JS. Static frame under reduced motion.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
