# Surface: marketing site (Home first, then every page in the same world)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: real estate agents and firms in Delhi NCR and Uttarakhand, mostly on phones. Action: send an enquiry (phone-first form) or open WhatsApp. Proof: sample renders, clearly labelled, until the owner supplies real footage. No invented stats, clients or testimonials. Replaces the earlier blue-hour world (owner: wide caps hard to read, look not premium).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is the lobby of a premium residential tower: a black stone wall with brushed-silver lettering, a tenant directory and a lift indicator. It refuses the category default of neon glow, wide tech caps and rows of icon cards.
OWN-WORLD: Black stone ground (#07080B) with slightly lighter slabs (#0E1015) joined by 1px steel hairlines. Silver lettering (#E4E7EC on #C3C8D0 body) in Archivo, mixed case: semi-expanded 500 for display, wide 300 for numerals, normal width for body. Electric blue (#1F7BFF / #59A8FF) is only the lit signal: lift LED, call button, focus, the active row. Buttons are flat rectangles with 4px corners, like lift call plates. No gradients, glows or swooshes on the wall; the swoosh lives only in the logo.
STORY: Visitors see their listings could look like this, find what Veylo does on the directory board (five services, five areas), and enquire or tap WhatsApp.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Left 55%: the headline in silver Archivo semi-expanded, three lines, about 5rem; one-line lede; WhatsApp ghost plate. Right: a tall framed display panel holding the lit tower render, captioned "Sample render". Full width under both: the band form (Name | Phone | Agent/Firm | blue call plate "Get my free content plan"). Header carries the lift indicator.
FORM: Tower lobby directory, candidate 5 of 7 on the grounded list, chosen by the owner. seed:02a90f0b093fc599
SIGNATURE: The lift indicator. A fixed indicator in the header counts floors G to 7 as you scroll (CSS scroll-driven animation of a registered integer property, no JS); the services directory reads as the tower's floors. Static "G" under reduced motion.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
