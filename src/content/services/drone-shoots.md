Location sells property, and nothing shows location like an aerial. One drone shot answers the question every buyer asks first: what is around it?

For townships, plots and hill properties, drone footage is the listing. For high-rises, it shows the view buyers are paying for.

**Best for:** plots, villas, township launches and any property where the view or the connectivity is the selling point.
